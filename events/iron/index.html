The Vision

A half-day reset, built for where you're at

Iron Sharpens Iron is a half-day gathering for entrepreneurial men: founders, coaches, and business owners. If that's you, expect physical nervous-system work (breathwork, sauna, cold plunge) combined with facilitated conversation and live social connection practice.

You're used to performing, for clients, for your team, for an audience. This is a space to set that down for an afternoon and connect with other men doing work like yours, in a setting built for that kind of openness.

What to Expect

Physical first, then facilitated

Your afternoon is split into two halves. The first half is physical: guided breathwork followed by sauna and cold plunge contrast therapy. The second half is facilitated: a talk on identity and self-perception, followed by a live, interactive session where you practice building genuine connections with the other men in the room.

You'll be in an intimate group, capped at 12 people, so the experience stays personal rather than performative.

Why It Matters

Regulated and known, in the same afternoon

There isn't much structured space for you, as an entrepreneurial man, to be physically regulated and genuinely known by your peers in the same afternoon. Most networking events are transactional. Most wellness spaces are solitary.

Iron Sharpens Iron gives you both: a real physical reset, plus real conversation and connection with people who understand the specific pressures of building something.
