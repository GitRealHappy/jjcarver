# The Living Room — A Connection & Reset Gathering for Entrepreneurs

Hosted by Jesse James Carver (The Living Internet Alliance) & Matthew Manning

## The Vision

The Living Room is a half-day gathering for entrepreneurs — founders, coaches, creators, and business owners. It combines physical nervous-system work (breathwork, sauna, cold plunge) with a facilitated talk and a structured practice in real connection.

The idea is simple. These are people used to performing — for clients, for teams, for an audience. This is a space to set that down for an afternoon and connect with other entrepreneurs doing similar work, in a setting built for that kind of openness.

## What to Expect

The day opens with guided introductions: a short facilitated round where everyone says plainly what they do, what they're building, and what they could use help with. That establishes real context before anything physical starts. From there, breathwork, then sauna and cold plunge become free-flow connection time — real conversation with no agenda, but with actual context behind it. The day closes with a talk on identity and self-perception, followed by a facilitated closing round.

It's an intimate, mixed group, capped at 10 participants (12 total including the two hosts), so the experience stays personal rather than performative.

## Why It Matters

There isn't much structured space for entrepreneurs to be physically regulated and genuinely known by peers in the same afternoon. Most networking events are transactional and most wellness spaces are solitary. The Living Room combines both: a real physical reset, plus real conversation and connection with people who understand the specific pressures of building a business.
