The Short Version

Jesse James Carver

I performed close-up and stage magic from the age of ten until I retired at fifteen. I have been fascinated by social psychology and the mysteries of perception ever since.

These days I write the Social Abundance letters, run The Living Internet Alliance's online community and in-person events, teach people to build one-person web design agencies, and occasionally record an episode of my podcast, Unblocked Creators. At heart I'm a simple artist. I find great joy in my contemplative woodworking hours.
