The Short Version

Jesse James Carver

I performed close-up and stage magic from the age of ten until I retired at fifteen. That is where I first learned that attention can be read and moved, and I have been fascinated by social psychology ever since.

These days I write the Social Abundance letters, run The Living Internet Alliance, teach people to build one-person web design agencies, and put out a podcast. When I am away from the screen I am in the shop, making tables, boards and wallets under the name Oakmoss.
